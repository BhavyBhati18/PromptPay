Advertiser Guidelines & Revenue Model

How Search Targeting Works

When users enter queries into ChatGPT or Google Gemini, our Ad Engine scans the prompt text for relevant keywords in real-time. If your ad matches the user's intent (e.g., prompt mentions "cloud hosting" and your ad has the "cloud" tag), your ad is prioritized for display.

Priced for Maximum ROI

Every impression costs only \$0.0003, which goes directly to rewarding the user for engaging with AI searches. Once users reach \$10.00, they withdraw their funds. This creates an authentic, win-win ecosystem where users welcome sponsored recommendations.
