Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 8, 2026

AI Search & Earn ("we", "our", or "us") provides a Chrome Extension that rewards users with micropayments ($0.0003 per search) when querying ChatGPT and Google Gemini, paired with non-intrusive sponsored ad recommendations.

1. Single Purpose

The single purpose of the Extension is to reward user search activity on supported AI platforms and display relevant sponsor placements based on search intent.

2. Data Collection & Use

We believe in minimal data collection:

  • Search Queries: Processed locally in the browser and transmitted to the ad engine solely to match relevant advertiser keywords and record the $0.0003 search reward.
  • Withdrawal Information: When requesting a payout at the $10.00 threshold, you voluntarily provide your PayPal address, crypto wallet, or bank account for payment fulfillment.
  • Anonymous Identifier: An anonymous UUID is assigned to your browser profile to track your balance and history.

4. Security

All data exchanged between the Extension and our server is encrypted using standard HTTPS/TLS protocols. We do not sell or monetize personal user data.
