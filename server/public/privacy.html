Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 8, 2026

QueryPay is committed to protecting your digital privacy. This policy outlines how information is collected, processed, and safeguarded when using our extension and website.

1. Data Collection & Purpose

We adhere to strict data minimization principles:

  • Search Queries: Processed in real-time solely to detect legitimate prompt submissions, credit your $0.0003 micropayment, and match sponsored campaign keywords.
  • Payout Details: Provided voluntarily when requesting a $10.00 withdrawal (PayPal email, crypto address, or bank details). Used strictly to deliver your funds.
  • Anonymous Identifier: An anonymous UUID is assigned locally to track your wallet balance and transactions.

2. What We Never Do

  • We NEVER record personal chat conversations, passwords, or credit card numbers.
  • We NEVER monitor activity outside of designated AI search domains (ChatGPT & Gemini).
  • We NEVER sell or broker personal browsing history to third parties.
